Keep it running

The gateway runs one of two ways: as a background LaunchAgent that survives a reboot, or only while the WeMessage window is open. The wizard asks you to pick, and you can change your mind with one command.

The service verbs live on the daemon. They are wemessaged, with a d, not the wemessage CLI you use for drafts and doctor. The CLI knows this and says so when you get it wrong, but it is the single most common paste error against this project, so it is stated here first rather than in a footnote.

Install the agent

wemessaged service install

Writes ~/Library/LaunchAgents/sh.wemessage.gateway.plist and hands it to launchd. From then on the gateway runs whether or not the window is open, and it comes back after a reboot and after a logout. The plist is a user LaunchAgent and nothing else: it runs as you, in your session, with your permissions, and it is never written into a system-wide location.

The plist is derived from where the daemon actually sits, so a bundle you moved is a bundle whose next install points at the new path rather than at a stale one.

Flags

  • --dir <path> the daemon's configuration directory.
  • --launch-agents-dir <path> where the plist is written.
  • --port <n> the port the agent's daemon listens on.
  • --no-load write the plist without handing it to launchd, for when you want to read it first.
  • --json machine-readable output, on every verb below as well.

Check on it

wemessaged service status --json

Reports whether the agent is loaded, which build the loaded plist points at, and where its state lives. This is the first thing to run when the app says the gateway is not answering.

Restart it

wemessaged service restart

The verb you want after an update, and the verb you want after re-adding WeMessage to Full Disk Access. A daemon that started before the grant was repaired is a daemon still holding the old answer. See permissions for why that grant needs repairing at all.

Remove it

wemessaged service uninstall

Unloads the agent and removes its plist. Your settings, drafts, and audit log are deliberately left where they are, because stopping a background service and erasing your data are different requests. Uninstall is the page for the second one.

Or run it only while the app is open

The LaunchAgent is a recommendation, not a requirement. If you would rather the gateway exist only while you are looking at it, skip the install: the app supervises the daemon itself and stops it on quit. Nothing is left running, and nothing starts at login.

The onboarding wizard asks you to choose between the two, and either answer is a supported configuration rather than a degraded one.

Where things are

  • The agent: ~/Library/LaunchAgents/sh.wemessage.gateway.plist
  • Logs: ~/Library/Logs/WeMessage
  • State: ~/Library/Application Support/WeMessage, unless WEMESSAGE_DIR says otherwise.
